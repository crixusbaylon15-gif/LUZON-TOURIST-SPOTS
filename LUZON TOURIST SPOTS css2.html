LUZON TOURIST SPOTS

Tourist Spots Activities/Things to enjoy

Masunggi Geoserve

 • Discovery Trail: A 3- to 4-hour ranger-guided trek across 60-million-year-old limestone karst terrain. Highlights include:
• Sapot: A giant metal spiderweb viewpoint suspended over jagged peaks.
• Duyan: A massive hanging hammock network floating high above the forest.
• Nanay and Tatay Decks: Scenic limestone peaks connected by rope bridges offering 360-degree views of the Upper Marikina River Basin.
• Yungib ni Ruben: An explored natural limestone cave along the path.
• Origin Trail: A fast-paced morning ascent featuring over 600 stone steps designed for cloud-level views and birdwatching.
• Legacy Trail: A hands-on immersion focused on tree nurturing and watershed restoration work.
• Garden Picnic: A relaxed 40-minute guided native tea tasting followed by a stroll through the Rock Garden

Hundred Islands

 • Pilgrimage Island: Visit the hilltop 56-foot Christ the Savior statue and enjoy a quiet, scenic walk.
• Cuenco Cave and Imelda Cave: Explore natural coastal caves and rock formations by foot or boat.
• Island-to-Island Zipline: Ride the 546-meter zipline stretching from Governor’s Island over to Virgin Island.
• Snorkeling and Helmet Diving: Discover shallow coral reefs and marine life around Quezon Island and Old Scout Island.
• Kayaking and Stand-Up Paddleboarding: Paddle through the calm emerald waters between nearby islets.
• Banana Boat amd Hurricane Rides: Enjoy high-speed water thrills towed by speedboats with friends.
• Cliff Jumping: Jump off rocky ledges into the deep, clear water at Marcos Island.
• Scuba Diving: Check out the vibrant underwater ecosystems and giant clam

Mt. Pulag

 • Catch the Sea of Clouds at Sunrise: Start a pre-dawn hike to reach the higher camps and the summit just as the sun rises above a thick blanket of clouds.
Trek the Major Trails: Choose a route that matches your skill level:
• Ambangeg Trail: The most popular and beginner-friendly route, taking about 4 to 6 hours.
• Akiki Trail: Known as the "killer trail" for experienced hikers looking for a steep, multi-day challenge.
• Tawangan and Ambaguio Trails: Longer, immersive forest-and-lakes or multi-province routes.
• Walk Through the Mossy Forests and Dwarf Bamboo Grasslands: Traverse distinct ecological zones packed with rare flora, moss-draped trees, and endemic species like the sweet edelweiss.
• Explore Kabayan Mummy Burial Caves: Visit nearby cultural and historical sites designated as national cultural treasures linked to the indigenous Ibaloi people.
